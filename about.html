A NOTE FROM OUR KITCHEN

Rooted in the
everyday.

Ora's Bakery is built around a simple idea: good baking brings people together. From a loaf picked up on the way home to a cake that takes centre stage at a celebration, food has a way of making moments feel special.

Our range brings together everyday bread, sweet treats, pastries, cakes and comforting drinks. We want choosing something delicious to feel easy, welcoming and worth coming back for.
